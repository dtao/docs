A timeline is the top-level container in Timeline Maker. It holds a complete set of milestones, a shared people list, and its own configuration — including date range and current-date setting. You can maintain as many timelines as you need, each representing a separate project, workstream, or planning horizon, and switch between them instantly from the sidebar.

## The Timeline Sidebar

The collapsible left sidebar is your home for navigating between timelines. It displays the total count of saved timelines and lists each one by name. Click any timeline in the list to load it into the main view. Collapse the sidebar to give the canvas more room when you are focused on a single timeline.

<Info>
  Each browser and device stores its own independent copy of your timelines. Timeline Maker saves data to browser localStorage, so timelines do not sync across devices or browsers automatically. If you need to move a timeline, use the SVG or PNG export as a portable snapshot.
</Info>

## What Each Timeline Contains

Every timeline you create is self-contained. Its configuration and data are stored together and do not affect any other timeline.

    A shared roster of team members available as owners when creating or editing milestones within this timeline.

## Managing Your Timelines

You can perform four operations on any timeline directly from the sidebar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new timeline">
    Click the **New Timeline** button at the top of the sidebar. Timeline Maker creates a blank timeline with a default name and opens it immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rename a timeline">
    Click the timeline's name in the sidebar to make it editable, type the new name, and confirm. The updated name is reflected across the sidebar and in localStorage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Duplicate a timeline">
    Use the duplicate action on an existing timeline to create an identical copy — milestones, people, and settings included. The copy appears in the sidebar with a modified name so you can tell it apart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete a timeline">
    Use the delete action to permanently remove a timeline and all its data. This action cannot be undone, so confirm before proceeding.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting a timeline removes it from localStorage immediately and permanently. There is no recycle bin or undo. Export a PNG or SVG snapshot first if you want to keep a record.
</Warning>

## Data Persistence

Timeline Maker stores all timeline data in your browser's localStorage under the key `timeline-maker-state-v1`. No account, server, or internet connection is required. Data persists across page refreshes and browser restarts as long as you do not clear site data for the page.

<Tip>
  If you use multiple browsers or work across devices, keep your timelines in sync by periodically exporting SVG snapshots. See the [Exporting guide](/guides/exporting) for details.
</Tip>
